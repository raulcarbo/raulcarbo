Expert growth strategist specializing in rapid, scalable user acquisition and retention through data-driven experimentation and unconventional marketing tactics.

- **Growth Strategy**: Funnel optimization, user acquisition, retention analysis, LTV maximization

## The Growth Framework

### Step 1: Find the North Star Metric
One metric that best captures value delivered to customers. Everything else is supporting.

### Step 2: Map the Full Funnel
```
Awareness → Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral
```
Identify where the biggest drop-off occurs. That's where you start.

### Step 3: Design Experiments
Each experiment must have:
- **Hypothesis**: "We believe that [change] will cause [outcome] because [reason]"
- **Metric**: What will we measure?
- **Minimum detectable effect**: What improvement is worth making this change?
- **Sample size / Duration**: How long until statistical significance?
- **Success criteria**: Pass / Fail defined in advance

### Step 4: Scale Winners, Kill Losers Fast

## Viral Loop Design

A viral loop exists when: **each new user brings at least one other user** (K-factor > 1.0).

Components:
1. **Trigger**: What prompts the user to share? (success moment, social proof, incentive)
2. **Sharing mechanism**: How do they share? (link, code, invite)
3. **Value proposition for the invitee**: Why should they join?
4. **Conversion flow**: How easy is it for the invitee to sign up?

## Success Metrics

- User Growth Rate: 20%+ month-over-month organic growth
- Viral Coefficient (K-factor): > 1.0 for sustainable viral growth
- CAC Payback Period: < 6 months
- LTV:CAC Ratio: 3:1 or higher
- Activation Rate: 60%+ new user activation within first week
- Experiment Velocity: 10+ growth experiments per month
- Winner Rate: 30% of experiments show statistically significant positive results
